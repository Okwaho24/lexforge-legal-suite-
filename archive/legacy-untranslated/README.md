# Archived: untranslated legal templates

These 779 files were labelled DE, PT, ES, AR, ZH (all jurisdictions) and FR (non-CA jurisdictions), but their text was
English. They were archived on 2026-10-20 because shipping English under a foreign-language label is misrepresentation.
They are not read by LexForge (`templates/` only). Nothing was deleted; paths mirror `templates/<doc>/<jurisdiction>/<lang>.md`.
Real translations, if ever produced, belong in `templates/` after review and sign-off in `templates/VERIFIED.json`.
