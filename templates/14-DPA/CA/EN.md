## 3. PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS

### 3.1 Compliance
Processor shall comply with PIPEDA, *The Health Information Protection Act* (Saskatchewan) where applicable, and all other applicable Canadian privacy legislation in performing its obligations under this DPA.

### 3.2 Confidentiality
Processor shall ensure that personnel authorized to process Personal Information are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

### 3.3 Security Measures
Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect Personal Information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, including:
(a) Encryption of Personal Information in transit and at rest;
(b) Access controls and multi-factor authentication for systems containing Personal Information;
(c) Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing;
(d) Employee training on data protection and security practices;
(e) Physical security measures for data centers and offices.

### 3.4 Data Subject Rights
Processor shall assist Controller in fulfilling Data Subject requests for access, correction, and withdrawal of consent under PIPEDA within {{DSR_RESPONSE|5}} business days of receiving a request from Controller.

## 4. SECURITY INCIDENTS

### 4.1 Notification
Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay, and in any event within {{INCIDENT_NOTICE|72}} hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Personal Information processed under this DPA.

### 4.2 Notification Content
Notification shall include: (a) nature of the Security Incident; (b) categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected; (c) categories and approximate volume of Personal Information affected; (d) likely consequences; (e) measures taken or proposed to address the incident.

### 4.3 Cooperation
Processor shall cooperate fully with Controller's investigation and remediation of any Security Incident and shall take all reasonable steps to mitigate harm to affected Data Subjects.

### 4.4 Regulatory Reporting
Controller is responsible for any required notifications to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or other regulatory authorities. Processor shall provide information and assistance as reasonably requested.
